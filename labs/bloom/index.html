Arrow keys move the brush ring, hold Shift to move faster. Enter paints a drop; hold Enter while moving to draw a line. Space plays or pauses. E toggles the eraser, S drops random seeds, and the square bracket keys change the brush size. Number keys 1 to 6 pick a recipe.

What is this?

Bloom runs the Gray–Scott model: two imaginary chemicals, A and B, spread across a grid. A is poured in everywhere at a steady feed rate, B eats A to make more of itself, and B is removed at a steady kill rate. Because A spreads twice as fast as B, tiny bumps snowball into coral, spots and stripes, and nudging feed or kill by a thousandth grows a completely different garden.
